PLAYER PROFILE

About Liam

Liam Stone is a Class of 2029 forward, winger and attacking midfielder from Augusta, Georgia. A versatile attacking player, Liam's strengths include his vision, passing, soccer IQ and ability to combine with teammates and create opportunities in the attack.

Primarily right-footed, Liam is also comfortable using his left and has experience playing across multiple attacking positions. He currently plays primarily as a forward for Augusta Arsenal after spending significant time on the wing.

Liam has been with Augusta Arsenal since age 9 and has represented Georgia ODP since 2023. A lifelong student of the game, he is committed to continuing his development both on the field and in the classroom.
